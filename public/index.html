How it works

  • Hyphal tips grow through the soil and digest what they touch. Growing costs food, so a colony in poor soil slows down.
  • Food travels home through the network, and some is lost on the way: the farther, the less arrives. Fused hyphae make shortcuts.
  • Reach a tree's roots and it trades sugar for your nutrients.
  • Lay a lure to steer the tips. Click passing earthworms.
  • When the soil is spent, fruit: the colony releases spores into a new forest.
